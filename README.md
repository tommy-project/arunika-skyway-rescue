ARUNIKA: SKYWAY RESCUE is a cinematic 3D arcade flight game: pilot the rescue aircraft Kestrel-7 through five beacon gates over a volcanic archipelago at golden hour, then land on cliffside Runway 7 as a storm rolls in.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080

## Controls

- W/S pitch, A/D bank, Q/E yaw
- Shift / Ctrl throttle up / down, Space airbrake
- Drag the mouse like a flight stick (down pulls up, right banks right)
- R restart, P or Esc pause, M mute

## Mission

Fly through Beacon 1 to 5 in order (+100 each, up to +50 centering bonus), grab the three optional gold supply rings (+25 each), then land on Runway 7. Landing is scored on alignment, descent rate and touchdown speed (up to +200), plus a smooth-flight bonus and a time bonus. Best score is saved in localStorage. Crashing into terrain or the ocean ends the mission.
